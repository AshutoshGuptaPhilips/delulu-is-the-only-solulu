# 📊 Pattern Insights Feature - User Guide

## Overview

**Pattern Insights** automatically analyzes your exercises to reveal recurring thought and behavior patterns. After completing at least 3 reality checks with reflections, you can explore AI-generated insights about your common thinking patterns.

---

## How It Works

### 1. **Requirements**
- Minimum 3 completed exercises
- Each exercise needs:
  - Event description
  - Emotion identified
  - Raw interpretation
  - Generated realities
  - User reflection

### 2. **Automatic Categorization**
Solulu automatically categorizes exercises into:

| Category | Triggered By | Examples |
|----------|-------------|----------|
| **Workplace** | Work-related keywords | boss, meeting, feedback, performance, project |
| **Relationship** | Relationship keywords | friend, partner, family, dating, argument |
| **Social** | Social situation keywords | party, crowd, embarrassment, anxiety, judgement |
| **General** | Everything else | Default category for mixed topics |

### 3. **Analysis Performed**
For each category, Solulu analyzes:

1. **What Happened** - Common themes in events
2. **Emotional Patterns** - Most frequent emotions
3. **Interpretation Patterns** - Cognitive distortion types
4. **Reality Check Impact** - Confidence shift before/after
5. **Key Insight** - AI-generated summary

## What Each Pattern Means

### Cognitive Distortions Tracked

**1. Catastrophizing**
- Assuming worst-case scenarios
- "Everything will go wrong"
- Example: "I made one mistake, my entire career is ruined"

**2. Mind Reading**
- Assuming you know what others think
- "They think negatively of me"
- Example: "Everyone in that meeting was judging me"

**3. Predicting Poor Outcomes**
- Being overly pessimistic about the future
- "Things won't work out"
- Example: "This won't go well, I know it"

**4. Taking Events Personally**
- Assuming you're responsible for everything
- "It's my fault"
- Example: "The project failed because I wasn't good enough"

**5. Assuming Exclusion**
- Believing you'll be left out or rejected
- "Nobody wants me there"
- Example: "They probably don't want me in the group"

**6. Overgeneralization**
- Making universal conclusions from single events
- "Always/Never" thinking
- Example: "I'll never be good at this"

## Interpreting Your Insights

### Confidence Shift
- **Large decrease** (30%+) = Reality checks significantly challenge your initial thoughts
- **Moderate decrease** (15-30%) = Reality checks provide some perspective shift
- **Small decrease** (<15%) = Your interpretations are relatively balanced already

### Emotional Patterns
- **High anxiety/fear** = Tendency toward threat perception
- **High frustration** = Pattern of feeling blocked or misunderstood
- **High insecurity** = Self-doubt and confidence concerns

### Theme Frequency
- **High frequency themes** = Your mind repeatedly focuses on these issues
- **Multiple themes** = Diverse concerns across situations
- **Single theme** = Focused anxiety about one issue type

## FAQ

### Q: I don't see Pattern Insights data
**A:** You need at least 3 completed exercises with reflections. Keep practicing!

### Q: Why is one category empty?
**A:** You haven't completed exercises in that category yet. The app tracks by keywords in your entries.

### Q: Can I change how exercises are categorized?
**A:** Not yet - categorization is automatic based on keywords. Future updates may allow manual categorization.

### Q: How often should I check Pattern Insights?
**A:** Monthly is ideal to track changes. Compare month-to-month to see progress.

### Q: What if my patterns don't match my experience?
**A:** The analysis is based on keywords in your writing. More detailed writing = more accurate analysis.

### Q: Can I export these insights?
**A:** Currently, you can screenshot. Export feature coming soon.

## Tips for Better Insights

### ✅ Do This:
- Write detailed descriptions of events
- Identify specific emotions accurately
- Include your honest catastrophic thoughts
- Write genuine reflections after reading realities
- Practice regularly (multiple exercises per week)

### ❌ Avoid:
- Very brief, vague descriptions
- Filtering emotions you think you "should" feel
- Minimizing negative thoughts
- Skipping reflections
- Only doing exercises occasionally
